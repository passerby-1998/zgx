01.

From Consumptive to Regenerative

Customers expect brands to address critical global issues and give back to people, planet and society. Brands must break the paradigm that loyalty drives unnecessary consumption, instead designing every new member experience to go beyond just reversing damage, and instead begin to shape a regenerative future.

  • Enabling more conscious decision-making
  • Rewarding sustainable behaviors and choices
  • Encouraging life-long circularity
  • Creating a movement for consumer purpose

71

%

of respondents

had favorable attitudes towards AI and its role in creating evolving, unique experiences for consumers

Source: Capgemini Customer Loyalty Survey, November 2023

02.

From Fragmented to Holistic

Traditionally, loyalty programs can be fragmented and disconnected from a brand's core experiences. Future membership offerings must be holistic and embedded across all touchpoints, acting as a golden thread that comprehensively addresses a broad spectrum of customer needs.

  • Connecting experiences across channels
  • Activating experiences throughout the ecosystem
  • Supporting customer data sharing and transparency
  • Empowering new business models and strategic growth

51

%

of respondents

stated that a connected, omnichannel loyalty experience was an important factor to make them loyal to a brand

Source: Capgemini Customer Loyalty Survey, November 2023

03.

From Static to Adaptive

With new technologies, brands will be empowered to deliver outstanding, hyper-personalized customer experiences with the scale, speed and consistency that consumers have grown to expect. As table stakes evolves, brands can leverage generative AI to quickly and impactfully adapt to changes in context, customers and channels.

  • Conversational customer journeys and engagement
  • Dynamic content and proposition generation
  • Elevating storytelling through personified brands
  • Evolving hyper-personalized experiences

75

%

of respondents

had favorable attitudes towards AI and its role in creating evolving, unique experiences for consumers based on sharing data and engagement behavior

Source: Capgemini Customer Loyalty Survey, November 2023

04.

From Individual to Collective

Brands that can create meaningful shared experiences for customers, and that can integrate community into new business initiatives, can benefit from serving the core human need to belong, while empowering their members to have a greater impact as part of a collective.

  • Co-creating with members and partners
  • Driving impact through community
  • Advocating for organic connection
  • Mobilizing new marketplaces for growth

73

%

of respondents

stated that they are more likely to buy from a brand if it makes them feel like they belong to a community

Source: Capgemini Customer Loyalty Survey, November 2023
