Social Impact

Design real solutions for communities in need

Using human-centered design to address the goals of communities in need
From global health to agriculture, disaster relief to financial inclusion, frog designs solutions, systems and strategies to address the needs and aspirations of underserved communities around the globe. Our teams leverage their experience helping industries across the globe deploy transformative strategies to ensure that the solutions we create are innovative, resilient and work for the people who need them most. We work locally and globally with philanthropies, NGO’s, B Corps, CSR’s, impact investors, and public/private partnerships to address challenges related to the UN Sustainable Development Goals, with an emphasis on financial inclusion, access to healthcare, and sustainability. frog’s Impact practice has the mission of applying our diverse and powerful abilities to social progress. Our Social and Environmental Impact work are necessarily deeply interconnected.
