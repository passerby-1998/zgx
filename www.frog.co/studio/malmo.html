Fun Facts
A Melting Pot of Cultures
With residents hailing from over 170 countries, Malmö is one of the most eclectic cities in Scandinavia. For the food loving frog, that blend is a dream come true: from Sweden's best falafel to Michelin-starred meals from locally-sourced produce, it's all here.
Young-Spirited
Of the 35,0000 people who live in Malmö, almost half are under the age of 35. It is most certainly a pond to get energized in.
Eurovision Hosts
In 2024, the grand final of the Eurovision Song Contest took place in the Malmö Arena, attracting people from all around the globe, who watched Switzerland bring home the gold medal.
