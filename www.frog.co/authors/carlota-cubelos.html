Carlota Cubelos

Researcher & Service Designer, frog, part of Capgemini Invent

Carlota is a Service Designer and Design Researcher at frog with over 10 years of experience at the intersection of design, research and sustainability. She helps organizations navigate complexity through human-centered research, systems thinking, and business strategy. Holding a PhD in Sustainability Science from The University of Tokyo and the United Nations University, she has worked across four continents with corporations, governments, startups and NGOs, translating insights about people, systems and emerging technologies into sustainable and meaningful futures.
