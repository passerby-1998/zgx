Ricky Hennessy

Senior data scientist

Ricky is a senior data scientist at frog Austin. He has a Ph.D. in biomedical engineering from UT, with expertise in scientific research, machine learning, algorithm development and data analysis, as well as experience with defense, finance, insurance and retail clients. At frog, he prototypes data-driven products and crafts intelligent data strategies. Ricky also teaches data science at General Assembly.
