Jenny Savage

Senior Visual Designer, frog, part of Capgemini Invent

Jenny Savage is a Senior Visual Designer and creative thinker at frog, where she’s spent more than a decade bringing together design strategy, storytelling, systems thinking, facilitation and emerging AI capabilities. She specializes in turning complex ideas into clear, human-centered experiences and helping teams move from vision to action. Curious and playful by nature, Jenny loves exploring neurodiversity and sharing POVs, mental models and unexpected connections that help people see familiar problems differently. She’s equally at home facilitating workshops, shaping design systems and visual identities or making comics in her spare time.
