Samara Watkiss

Associate Design Director, sustainable Futures

Samara is an Associate Design Director with a background in user research, interaction and service designer and a facility to work cross-culturally (whether that be in another country or embedded in a client team). Samara brings strategic and creative problem solving together to achieve business goals, and improve experiences for users and employees.

Prior to joining the Sustainable Futures group, Samara was the Sustainability Lead for the Austin Studio. She has expertise in the energy sector, circular economy, digital sustainability and climate story-telling. She is passionate about creating products and services that, in the words of William McDonough, allow us to “love all the children of all the species for all time.”
