DE&I

Creating a more diverse, equitable and inclusive world through design

Doing our part to ensure a more equitable and inclusive world within our company, our client work, our communities and beyond
Our Mission
In order to truly advance the human experience through design, we must have a deep understanding of the diversity of cultures, backgrounds, needs and emotions that shape our world. We are wholeheartedly committed to creating a diverse, equitable and inclusive working environment for all frogs, and to co-creating products, services and experiences that represent and serve our global community. We believe that diversity and inclusion yields game-changing results that touch hearts and move markets, is essential to our strategic growth objectives, and is how we will make our mark on the future.
Our Commitment
In order to fulfill our mission to design a more diverse, equitable and inclusive world, we also need to be transparent and real about the work we have yet to do from the inside out. The first step, like with any frog project, was to understand the problems and pain points in order to come up with solutions—not just empty statements. As a result, our Diversity, Equity & Inclusion Committee, made up of frogs from across our global studios, has identified the following commitments to help us achieve a more equitable company, community and world.
Showing up in the World
Speak out against systemic racism and oppression and work to dismantle it in our field and the world.
Showing up within frog
Celebrate and support frogs and partners of all backgrounds, perspectives and identities.
Education & Training
Listen to, train and educate our frogs so they are empowered to impact diverse, equitable and inclusive spaces.
Hiring
Champion equitable hiring practices and cultivate a diversified talent pipeline.
Development & Retention
Create equitable growth opportunities to increase diversity across all levels.

Our Partnerships
frog is made up of passionate dreamers and curious rebels—both within our studios and our global community partners. Below are some of the conferences, organizations and events we’ve collaborated with to promote diversity, equity and inclusion in and beyond design.
ADCOLOR
Celebrating diversity in the creative and technology industries, Adcolor is a membership organization facilitating programs and initiatives that strengthen diversity retention in hiring.
Lesbians Who Tech
A three-day summit providing opportunities for networking and recruiting in support of the LGBTQ tech community.
The 3%
The 3% brings together creative industry professionals for two days of talks, classes and networking on the importance of diversity to creativity.
MAIP
The Face of Talent is an event held over 5 days in NYC and includes diversity leadership networking, a career fair, awards ceremony and an immersion day.
