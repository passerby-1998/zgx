Digital Strategy Senior Consultant

Why Join frog?

Since June 2021, frog is part of Capgemini Invent. frog partners with customer-centric enterprises to drive sustainable growth, by building and orchestrating experiences at scale, while harnessing the power of data and technology.  We’re inventing the future of customer experiences by delivering market-defining business models, products, services, brand engagements and communications.

Joining frog means you’ll be joining the “pond,” a global network of studios, each with a thriving in-person and vibrant virtual culture. frogs are curious, collaborative, and courageous, united by our passion for improving the human experience across our areas of expertise, while each bringing our unique and diverse skills and experiences to the table. We draw on our global reach and local knowledge to solve complex problems and create innovative, sustainable solutions that touch hearts and move markets. frogs prize humour, positivity, and community just as highly as performance and outcomes. Our culture is open, flexible, inclusive, and engaging. Working at frog means being empowered to meet the moment, and Make Your Mark on every project, in your studio, your community—and the world at large.

Digital Strategy

Digital Strategy sits at the heart of Business Reinvention at frog. Our team harnesses customer and commercial insight alongside the power of data and technology – defining the business models, products, services and brand experiences of tomorrow.

We are seeking to grow our practice by a Digital Strategist into our team – at a Senior Consultant level. We are especially interested in applicants who have some experience within (but not necessarily limited to) the Energy & Utilities sector and other regulated industries.

An Overview Of The Role

As a Digital Strategist at frog you’ll be passionate about the amazing opportunities that are opening up for businesses in the AI era – and excited to roll up your sleeves and help our clients realise this untapped potential.

You’ll operate at the intersection of strategy, product, data and technology — translating complex insight into clear, actionable direction. You’ll help senior clients (up to C-level) identify, prioritise and realise value from digital and AI investments. You’ll shape end-to-end propositions, from insight to business case to implementation roadmap.

You’ll be a polymath – as comfortable drafting a commercial model and business case as you are empathising with the needs of our clients and their customers. You’ll thrive on your ability to weave a transformative story out of a mountain of insight, data and competing perspectives. And you’ll collaborate seamlessly across frog’s multi-disciplinary team of world-class designers, technologists, marketers and more.

As a Senior Consultant in our team:

  • You will contribute independently at a high level on client engagements, owning important workstreams within these projects
  • You will contribute actively to our team outside of client engagements e.g. sharing skills and perspectives with team members, supporting on BD efforts, supporting leaders with aspects of team management and other internal initiatives
  • You will have  previous experience in relevant roles

What We Look For

For these roles we’re looking for experienced strategy practitioners. You will likely have previous  experience in strategy consulting, investment banking, corporate strategy and innovation, or a related field.  We recognize the best strategists come from a variety of backgrounds, so most importantly, you have a proven ability to think commercially, strategically, and creatively — don’t let job titles and degrees hold us back from meting you!

We don't expect you to hit all of the elements below perfectly, but we do want to be sure that you will bring valuable skills, experience and creative energy into our team:

Strategic thinking & action – You have strong problem solving and analytical skills, with the ability to move beyond theory to identify practical recommendations that can be implemented by our clients

Commercial modelling – You are highly numerate and have experience owning detailed assumptive models that quantify the size of the prize and path to value for our clients

Strategic storytelling – You are comfortable building and presenting compelling narratives and business cases, using words and numbers with precision to communicate the business value of our work and recommendations

Deep research – You are competent across a range of research methods and lenses, such as qual & quant customer research, market & competitor analysis, stakeholder interviews, SWOT & PESTLE analysis etc.

Product & business model innovation – You are able to translate research & analysis into forward-facing customer propositions and product strategies that bridge from where our clients are today to where they can be in future

AI and emerging technologies – You have practical understanding of how AI and emerging technologies translate into real business value, with experience applying these in strategy, product or transformation contexts

Stakeholder engagement & communication – You are confident engaging with stakeholders at all levels, over email, on calls and in meetings or workshops

Outside of the specific skillsets attached to this role, we look for individuals who are well aligned to our culture and ways of working at frog:

Pragmatic problem-solver – Focuses on finding practical solutions and reducing complexity to drive results.

Collaborative team player – Builds strong relationships and works effectively across diverse stakeholders and across craft disciplines in frog/Capgemini.

Excellent interpersonal skills – Builds trust and fosters strong connections with colleagues, clients, and stakeholders.

Adaptable and agile – Thrives in dynamic environments and adjusts to shifting priorities with ease.

Growth mindset and curiosity - You’re always learning, experimenting with new technologies, and pushing the boundaries of what’s possible.

It would be a bonus if you have

  • Energy & Utilities sector experience
  • Experience applying strategy in regulated industries
  • Both highly desirable – but should not dissuade you from applying if you don’t have them!

Need To Know

We don’t just believe in inclusion, we actively go out to making it a working reality. Driven by our core values and Inclusive Futures for All campaign, we build environments where you can bring you whole self to work.

We aim to build an environment where employees can enjoy a positive work-life balance. We embed hybrid working in all that we do and make flexible working arrangements the day-to-day reality for our people. All UK employees are eligible to request flexible working arrangements.
