Experience Design Intern

Why join frog?

Since June 2021, frog is part of Capgemini Invent. We partner with customer-centric enterprises to drive sustainable growth, by building and orchestrating experiences at scale, while harnessing the power of data and technology. We’re inventing the future of brands and customer experiences by delivering market-defining business models, products, services, branding and communications.

Joining frog means you’ll be joining the “pond,” a global network of studios, each with a thriving in-person and vibrant virtual culture. frogs are curious, collaborative, and courageous, united by our passion for improving the human experience through design, while each bringing our unique and diverse skills and experiences to the table. We draw on our global reach and local knowledge to solve complex problems and design innovative, sustainable solutions that touch hearts and move markets. frogs prize humour, positivity, and community just as highly as performance and outcomes. Our culture is open, flexible, inclusive, and engaging. Working at frog means being empowered to Make Your Mark on every project, in your studio, your community—and the world at large.

Your Role

Experience Designers create digitally enabled products and services, applying skills across interaction design, visual design, and service design to develop holistic experiences from early concepts to detailed solutions. You will support teams on client-facing projects, pitches and internal initiatives.

You will learn by doing, with guidance from experienced practitioners who will help you understand frog’s methodologies and deliver high-quality work in the context of interdisciplinary projects.

Project teams rely on Experience Designers to:

  • Create digital design artefacts including user flows, UI mock-ups, prototypes, journey maps, service blueprints, and ecosystem maps to communicate product behaviour, experience concepts, and complex systems, including products in the GenAI space.
  • Analyse and benchmark existing solutions, identify pain points and opportunity spaces, and conduct foundational research and usability testing to generate actionable insights and inform design directions.
  • Collaborate with cross-disciplinary teams to define project requirements by balancing user needs, market opportunities, business goals, and technical feasibility, while creating high-quality digital product experiences.
  • Execute detailed design concepts using strong interaction and visual design principles, applying holistic critical thinking and contributing across disciplines and workstreams.
  • Support successful project delivery through persuasive presentations, clear documentation, and effective handover of outcomes to clients.

Your Profile:

  • Business-level fluency in both English and German is required. Only in exceptional cases of candidate with outstanding skills, are we considering non-German speaking applicants, alongside a recently completed a university degree in Interaction Design, HCI, or a related design discipline (we also accept interns who are in the middle of a Masters degree) 
  • Strong digital design skills with expert use of Figma, with knowledge of interaction patterns, information architecture, user flows, wireframes, and product logic across mobile and desktop platforms alongside knowledge of Design System, how to use and them and contribute to it. 
  • Experience using GenAI tools for design, prototyping, visual concept creation, storytelling materials, and ideally designing, prototyping, or building AI-native products such as voice-based, agentic, or hyper-personalized experiences. 
  • Ability to rationalize design decisions, communicate work clearly through strong presentation design and storytelling, and collaborate effectively with design, business, and technology teams in iterative environments. 
  • Adaptability, willingness to quickly learn new domains and client challenges, and a strong affinity with frog’s values and human-centred product design.

It would be a bonus if you had any one of the following:

  • Experience in 2D motion design - animating or editing using After Effects, Adobe Premiere Pro, 3D modelling and 3D motion design (Blender, Rhino, Unity, Keyshot etc.) to showcase product and experiential stories to clients.
  • Experience in Brand and Visual Identity, crafting brand narratives, brand experiences and brand strategy, building a Corporate Identity or physical branded interactions.

What you’ll love:

  • Join a leading international design consultancy that shapes the future through innovative, groundbreaking projects, global teams, and strong digital expertise
  • Drive innovative transformation initiatives in forward-looking industries and work on visionary, strategic developments
  • Grow in a culture-driven environment shaped by our people, legacy and rituals
  • Benefit from our comprehensive offerings for an optimal work-life balance, including health & wellbeing programs, mobility packages, and modern office spaces

We look forward to receiving your complete application via our application portal. Please include a CV in English and a link or PDF of your portfolio of recent work and earliest possible start date.

  • Applicants should be available for six months, ideally starting the beginning of October
  • Candidates should be eligible to legally work and live in Germany. We do not support visa applications for internship roles.

If you have any questions, please contact paulina.misztal@capgemini.com.

About Capgemini

It delivers end-to-end services and solutions leveraging strengths from strategy and design to engineering, all fueled by its market leading capabilities in AI, cloud and data, combined with its deep industry expertise and partner ecosystem.
