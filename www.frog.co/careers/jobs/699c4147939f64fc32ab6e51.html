(Senior) Manager Platforms & Prototypes

Are you passionate about unlocking the value of customer data and shaping AI-powered experiences? Do you want to help organizations prepare and thrive in a future where eco and digital rule together? So do we! We’d love to have you in our pond at frog.

We are frog, part of Capgemini Invent, and we are the reinvention and experience partner for the eco-digital era. We’re in it for the long run. Together with our clients, we shape the future by making customer data and technology tangible, impactful, and human.

If you join our team, you become part of the Platforms & Prototypes Team. This is a team we are scaling up with a great pool of talents. So if you want to become part of a group of builders, shapers and do’ers this is the place for you. Our team will consist out of a mix of seniority and specialisms: strategists, designers, CX-tech experts, data scientists and vibe-coders.

What you do

As a Manager in the Platforms & Prototypes team you own delivery and value realisation for our MarTech and CX-Tech engagements. You orchestrate multidisciplinary squads, lead delivery of projects and act as the trusted advisor who bridges C-suite ambition, customer value and composable technology.

· Lead discovery through scale-up: futurecasting CX, frame business cases, design capability roadmaps and advise on CX/MarTech/AI platforms like Adobe, Salesforce or Google.

· Co-create and launch new data-driven digital propositions: from loyalty schemes to D2C subscriptions. Validating hypotheses through rapid MVPs and in-market experiments.

· Drive Gen-AI adoption: prioritise use-cases, govern data ethics & GDPR, and embed real-time decisioning into marketing, sales and service journeys.

· Manage commercials: shape pursuits, scope contracts and protect margin while delivering measurable customer and business impact.

· Mentor consultants and senior consultants; cultivate a learning culture centred on experimentation, inclusivity and innovation in the eco-digital era.
