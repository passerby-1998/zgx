Envisioning the next-generation of financial products, services and paradigms

Banking on Invisibility

Previously, banks had a prime position on the high street. Now, that’s wavering. In the future they may not even have a position in the phone app. We're witnessing the rise of next-gen banking models, along with a wave of novel products and services that are tailor-made for a world with dissolving boundaries.

This report represents a multi-faceted response to the ever-shifting financial services landscape. In it, industry experts explore next-generation banking models, outlining how to unlock emerging opportunity areas—for financial institutions and consumers alike.

Covered in this report:  

  • Successful approaches to win in invisible finance 

  • Three interlocking strategies to elevate CX 

  • Insights from global leaders into key industry challenges
