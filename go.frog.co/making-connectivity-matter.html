Making Connectivity Matter

Future-proofing intelligent products and services

This latest installment of frog’s Chief Challenges series is an exploration into our Connected World. As the climate crisis comes into clearer focus, the interwoven nature of the planet’s systems and populations is becoming ever more apparent—and we’re increasingly aware that we must level up on our own connectedness.

Against a backdrop of increasing product and service complexity, plus a competitive landscape that’s changing quickly in this age of AI, companies face a daunting path to transform and launch intelligent products and services. The bar to creating new offerings may be rising, but so too is the potential payoff.

Covered in this report:  

  • Four interlocking strategies for connected products and services

  • Insights from global leaders into key industry challenges

  • Cutting-edge client stories
