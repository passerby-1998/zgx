Conversations on the future of mobility

The Road Ahead

Mobility is set to shift into a new gear. Emerging technology, changing business models and an emphasis on sustainability is transforming the way consumers and automotive manufacturers alike imagine and experience the ride.

In The Road Ahead, frog’s new downloadable report, we examine the wide-open opportunities coming to the future of mobility. Inside, find insights from conversations with experts in the world of business, innovation, academia and art.

Covered in this Report: 

  • New mobility ecosystems 

  • Autonomous futures 

  • Refueling vs. recharging 

  • Vehicles unbundled 

  • Designing a ‘Harmless Car’
