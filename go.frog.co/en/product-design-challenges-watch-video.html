Launching Products in Uncertain Times

A Panel at SxSW 2021, hosted by frog

Now more than ever before, bringing game-changing digital products to market quickly requires clear methodology. COVID lockdowns and new remote work environments have only increased challenges in an already-tight market, but some leaders have turned those challenges into opportunities to re-think the way they work—especially when it comes to product design and delivery.

For this year's virtual SXSW festival, we gathered some of the top experts in the product design and delivery space—all of whom happen to be women leaders—to discuss how they’ve managed to rise above the challenges of the moment and bring great ideas to life.
